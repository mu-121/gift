Warda is busy — and she is beautiful

Duniya chalti rahe, main aap ke paas rahun.

Warda Nayab’s days move fast — work, thoughts, duty, and a soft smile. Being busy is part of her story. This page is the same truth that lives at home: she keeps going, and Muhammad Usman walks with her step .

He stays with her

Main saath hoon. Help roz ka pyar hai.

Usman is not only watching . When Warda is busy, he is right there — holding her hand , making the load lighter, saying “I am here” without a lot of noise.

Chai, made with love

Ek cup chai, poora din naram.

After tired hours , or in the middle of a talk — Usman makes chai. Steam , a cup, and that moment when Warda pauses. This small ritual brings them back to each other.

  • Garam cup, naram pause.
  • One sip, then the day feels lighter.

Dinner at home
